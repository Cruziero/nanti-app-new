NANTI UI/UX REDESIGN — CRAFT-INSPIRED WORKSPACE


IMPORTANT:


I do NOT like the current NANTI UI.


The current interface feels too much like a generic AI SaaS dashboard with many cards, statistics and green accents.


I want to redesign the entire authenticated NANTI application.


Use the design philosophy and information architecture quality of Craft as inspiration:


https://www.craft.do/


IMPORTANT:
Do NOT clone Craft.
Do NOT copy their branding.
Do NOT copy their exact layouts.
Do NOT copy their visual assets.


Instead, borrow these principles:


- content-first interface
- quiet premium design
- excellent whitespace
- strong sidebar navigation
- workspace feeling
- minimal visual noise
- subtle borders
- typography-driven hierarchy
- elegant navigation
- contextual panels
- document-like content
- information hierarchy instead of dashboard cards
- app should feel like a place users live in every day


NANTI must remain completely original.
