## HIGH (Hard Gate)

### A-01 — Em dash in copy (R-02)

The em dash character (`—`) appears throughout user-facing titles, meta descriptions, and body copy. It is an absolute ban in output.

Locations:
- `src/routes/index.tsx:9,15,25` (page title, og:title, twitter:title)
- `src/config/site.ts:134,136,264,272,285,301`
- `src/config/homepage.ts:98,100,101,103`
- `src/routes/business.tsx:64`
- `src/routes/personal.tsx:72`
- `src/routes/pricing.tsx:10`
- `src/routes/about.tsx:9`
- `src/data/articles.ts:28,59,88`
- `src/routes/api/cron/generate-article.tsx:71`
- `src/routes/legal.privacy.tsx:38,60,63,66,69`

Reason: R-02 forbids the em dash in any text. Replace with a period, comma, colon, or parentheses.
Note: `legal.privacy.tsx:1` and `legal.terms.tsx:1` are code comments, out of scope here.

### A-02 — False security claim: "End-to-end encrypted" (R-36)

Locations: `src/routes/index.tsx:605`; `src/config/site.ts:202`.
The product is a web app that sends shared conversation text to Google Gemini and stores data in Supabase. It is not end-to-end encrypted. This is a fabricated security claim and a Hard Gate failure.

### A-03 — Unverifiable "we never store raw conversation" claims (R-36, R-38)

Locations: `src/routes/index.tsx:606,607`; `src/config/site.ts:202`; `src/data/articles.ts:169-181`.
Copy says raw conversation text is deleted immediately and never stored, but the Privacy Policy (`legal.privacy.tsx:78-83`) says data is retained while the account is active. The two contradict. State only what is actually true, or remove the claim.

### A-04 — Fabricated testimonial (R-18)

Locations: `src/config/site.ts:183-188`; `src/config/homepage.ts:164-171`.
"NANTI helped me stop losing promises in WhatsApp." attributed to "Tom, Owner, PT Maju Jaya" is invented. No real testimonial exists, so the testimonial must be removed (not rewritten).

### A-05 — "AI-powered" / "AI Memory" buzzwords (R-16)

Locations: `src/routes/legal.terms.tsx:39` ("AI-powered workspace"); `src/routes/index.tsx:94` + `src/config/site.ts:139` + `src/config/homepage.ts:106` ("AI Memory for WhatsApp"); `src/data/articles.ts:32,75`.
"AI Powered" is explicitly forbidden. Replace with a specific description of what the product does.

### A-06 — Generic CTAs "Get started" / "Contact us" (R-15)

Locations: `src/config/site.ts:254,275,296,313,329,345`.
These render on `/pricing` (via `loadConfig`) and in the admin-editable config. "Get Started" is a forbidden default CTA. Use the specific action (for example, "Create a free account", "Talk to us about a team plan").
